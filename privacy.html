Privacy policy

Senpai Secrets Uploader (Google / YouTube)

Our uploader app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel. It does not access, store or share any other user's Google or YouTube data, and it requests only the permission to upload videos. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Websites

Our pages collect nothing that identifies you. Votes and idea suggestions on our fan site are anonymous. We use no advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share data.
